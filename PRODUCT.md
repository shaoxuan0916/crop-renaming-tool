# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People preparing batches of cropped and consistently named teaching or document images on a desktop.

## Product Purpose

Turn PDFs and images into an orderly, renamed WebP export without sending source files to a server.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, precise, trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Generic AI dashboards, decorative gradients, neon accents, glass effects, and color used without meaning.

## Design Principles

- Keep the workflow visually obvious.
- Use color for actions and state, not decoration.
- Make dense batch work calm and legible.
- Preserve the browser-local trust signal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Maintain strong text and control contrast in both themes, visible focus states, reduced-motion support, and status cues that do not rely on color alone.
